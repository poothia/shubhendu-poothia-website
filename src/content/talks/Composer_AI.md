Presented at Red Hat One Global 2025 this talk explored moving beyond model training and tuning to build a complete enterprise AI application lifecycle. The session focused on leveraging Red Hat OpenShift and OpenShift AI (RHOAI) to provide a scalable, end-to-end platform for secure AI application development.

The presentation introduced Red Hat Composer AI (RHCAI) to streamline generative AI integration through prompt engineering, guardrails, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), and agentic workflows. It highlighted a practical healthcare prior authorization use case demonstrating multi-agent orchestration with graph/vector databases and open-source models like IBM Granite.
